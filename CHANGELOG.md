# Changelog

## v0.1.0-beta.1

First beta.

- "Read Aloud" button on quest offers, quest turn-ins, the quest log (map panel and popup), NPC dialogue, and books, plaques and letters.
- Reads only the story text: titles and descriptions, not objectives or rewards.
- The button turns into "Stop" while reading. Clicking Read Aloud somewhere else switches to the new text.
- Keeps reading after you accept or complete a quest; stops when you close the window or open something else.
- Options panel (Options → AddOns → Lorecaster) with voice, speed, volume and a test button. Also `/lore` slash commands.
- Works in WoW Forever and retail.
